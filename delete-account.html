Delete your RemindGuard account and data

This page is for the app RemindGuard: Tasks & Habits by Md. Ayatullah Siddique (Ayatullah Tech).

Delete it yourself inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open RemindGuard and sign in with the Google account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings and open Search, history & backup.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE in the box and confirm.

The account and its data are deleted straight away and you are signed out. This cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email address, profile photo).
  • Your cloud backup: tasks, habits, document notes, family members, settings and history.
  • Your plan and coupon records, and your sign-in account.
  • Data stored only on your phone (including activity routes) is removed when you delete the app or clear its data in your phone settings.

What is kept

  • Purchase and billing records held by Google Play. They are managed by Google, not by RemindGuard. To stop a subscription, cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Cannot open the app?

Email ayatullah.akash@gmail.com from the email address of the account, with the subject "Delete my RemindGuard account". We will delete the account and its data and reply to confirm.
