2. What RemindGuard does

RemindGuard is a personal reminder tool. It lets you:

  • Save document notes — the name of a document and its important dates (for example an expiry date). You can type the dates in yourself, or photograph a document and let the app read the dates into a draft note. The app never stores the document or its photo: the photo is deleted once you confirm the extracted dates, and only your confirmed note is kept.
  • Create tasks with one-off or repeating reminders (daily, on chosen weekdays, or every few days), at one or more times of day.
  • Track habits (paid feature) with goals, check-ins and streaks.
  • Organise items under family member profiles that you create and manage.
  • Receive reminders as push notifications at the times you set, in your phone's local time.

The app is a memory aid. It does not replace your official documents, and it is not a government, legal, medical, or financial service.

  • Do not rely on RemindGuard as your only safeguard for critical deadlines. Notifications depend on your device: they can be delayed or blocked by battery-saving settings, notification permissions, “Do Not Disturb”, time-zone changes, or the device being off or out of coverage.   • You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you enter, and for backing up anything you cannot afford to lose (a paid export feature is provided for this).
