1. Who we are

RemindGuard (“RemindGuard”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) is developed and operated by Md. Ayatullah Siddique (Ayatullah Tech), reachable at ayatullah.akash@gmail.com, Talsar, Jhenaidah 7330, Bangladesh. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

2. What RemindGuard does

RemindGuard helps you and your family keep track of document expiry dates (for example passports, ID cards, licences and insurance), everyday tasks, and habits. You save the important dates as short notes, set reminders, and the app notifies you before a date arrives. Reminders are delivered as push notifications on your phone, based on your phone's local time.

3. The most important fact: we never store your documents

RemindGuard does not store document photos or document files — anywhere. When you photograph a document, the app reads the dates from the photo and shows them to you as a note. Only after you check the dates and confirm they are correct is the photo deleted. The photo is never kept in the app as a stored document, and you can always skip the photo entirely and type the dates in yourself (manual entry).

What the app keeps is only the note you confirm: the document's name, the dates, and any short text you add.

4. Information the app handles

4.1 Notes, tasks and habits you create

  • Document notes — a title (for example “Passport — Ayesha”), issue and expiry dates, an optional renewal note, and the family member the note belongs to.
  • Tasks — task names, reminder times, and repeat patterns you set (daily, weekly on chosen days, or every few days, for example medicine every 3 days).
  • Notes — text or checklists you write, with a colour, an optional pin, an optional reminder time (the app rings an alarm then) and an archive flag.
  • Routines (paid feature, also called habits) — routine names, goals, schedules, check-ins, streaks and notes you record.
  • Family member names — names or labels you type in for the people whose documents and tasks you track. Adding members is optional; a single user can keep everything under their own profile.
  • Settings — your language, theme, reminder-assistant style, plan, and preferences.

4.2 Google sign-in information

If you sign in with Google, the app receives only your Google account's profile name, email address, and profile photo, and shows them in the app (for example in the header and Settings) so you can see which account you are using. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, photos, or any other Google account content. Signing in is handled by Google; Google's own privacy policy applies to that step.

You can also open the app without signing in (“view without login”) to look around a read-only demo. In that guest/demo view no personal information is collected from you, and actions such as adding or editing are disabled until you sign in.

4.3 Subscription information

If you buy a paid plan, the purchase itself is processed by Google Play. We receive and keep your plan status (Free, Essential, Pro or a custom document plan), its end date and a Google Play purchase token, so the app can unlock the right features, show you when your plan ends, and keep it correct when Google Play reports a renewal, cancellation or refund. We do not receive or store your card or bank details. Paid plan prices depend on your country and are shown by Google Play at checkout (see the Terms of Service).

4.5 Location (Activity tracking only)

If you choose to record an Activity, RemindGuard asks for your phone's location permission and uses your precise location, only while a recording is running, to draw the route and work out distance, pace and speed. A notification is shown while it runs. The route and results are kept in your activity history on your phone only: they are not uploaded to our servers, not part of your account backup, and not shared with anyone unless you tap Share on a card. You can stop recording at any time, delete any activity from the history, or turn the permission off in your phone settings; the rest of the app works without it.

4.4 Information we do not collect

  • No document photos or files (see section 3).
  • No background location tracking. Location is used only while you record an Activity (walk, run, cycle and similar) with the Start button; see section 4.5. Reminder times simply follow your phone's local clock and time zone.
  • No contacts, call logs, messages, or browsing history.
  • No advertising identifiers. RemindGuard shows no regular ads and contains no advertising SDKs.

4.6 Account and device information

When you are signed in, your account record on our servers also holds: the country code of the country your phone is in (from the SIM card or mobile network, otherwise the time zone), used to show the right plan prices and to count users by country; the time you last opened the app; a random session code that keeps your account signed in on one phone at a time (signing in on a new phone signs the old one out); and, if notifications are on, a message token for your phone (a random code from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) so that we can send you messages. The message token is removed when you sign out. None of this is used for advertising.

The account record also holds your invite code (a short random code you can share with friends), the id of the person who invited you if you used their code, and how many friends joined with your code. If the app stops working (a crash), anonymous crash reports are sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics: technical details such as the phone model, Android version, app version and what the app was doing when it failed, together with a random installation id. They contain no names, notes, tasks or documents, and they are used only to find and fix problems.

5. Where your information is stored

Your notes, tasks, habits and settings are stored on your own phone, in the app's local storage on the device. When you are signed in, a copy of them is also saved as a private backup on our servers (Google Firebase), so you can restore them on a new phone. Only you can read that backup. RemindGuard works without an internet connection: everything you create is saved on your phone first, and reminders and alarms ring from the phone itself. Whatever was changed while you were offline is sent to your private backup automatically the next time the phone is online. You can also export a backup file and save or share it yourself, to a place you choose; if you later restore from such a file, you import a file that you hold.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's data in your phone's settings, deletes the locally stored information from that device.

6. How information is used

  • To show your document notes, tasks, habits and countdowns inside the app.
  • To send the reminder notifications you scheduled, at the times you set, showing the task or document name.
  • To display your signed-in profile (name, email, profile photo).
  • To apply your plan's limits and features.
  • To tell you when your plan starts, changes or ends, and to remind you two days before a Google Play subscription renews.
  • To show a short summary of your day (your tasks and documents expiring soon) every morning at 8:00. It is made on your phone from the data on your phone, and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • To run the invite-a-friend offer (each of you gets 7 days of Pro) and to prevent misuse of it.
  • To find and fix crashes, with the anonymous crash reports described in section 4.6.
  • To remind you to come back: if you have not opened the app for about 3 or 7 days, your phone shows a short message (this is set on the phone itself; nothing is sent from a server for it).
  • To send you occasional messages about RemindGuard (for example offers or news) to your phone. For this, the message token described in section 4.6 is used. You can stop all of these messages by turning off notifications for the app in your phone settings.
  • To keep the app working correctly (for example, restoring your settings after an update).

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Information is only involved with third parties in these limited ways:

  • Google Sign-In — Google provides the sign-in screen and passes us the profile name, email and profile photo described above.
  • Google Play — Google processes paid purchases and provides us with your plan status.
  • Google Firebase — our servers run on Google Firebase (sign-in, database, server functions, message delivery and crash reports). It processes the information in sections 4.2, 4.6 and 5 on our behalf, under Google's terms.
  • Your own exports — if you export a backup and send it somewhere (for example to your own cloud drive or email), that destination's privacy policy applies to your copy.
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, a court order, or to protect the rights and safety of users.

8. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — only when you choose to photograph a document so the app can read its dates. The photo is deleted after you confirm the extracted dates (section 3). You can deny this permission and use manual entry instead.
  • Notifications — so reminders can be delivered as push notifications. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings at any time; the app will still keep your notes and dates.
  • Alarms & reminders, and full-screen alerts — so that reminders ring at the exact minute, even when the phone is idle or locked. The phone may show its alarm icon while a reminder is waiting. You can change these in your phone settings; reminders may then arrive late.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock (optional) — only if you turn on "App lock" in Settings: the app asks your phone to unlock it. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint or face; this setting stays on your phone.
  • Location (optional) — only while you record an Activity (section 4.5).
  • Internet — to sign in, keep your backup, check purchases and plan prices, and receive messages. The app's main features work without it.
  • Microphone (optional, where offered) — only while you use voice input to dictate a task or note. You can deny it and type instead.

9. How long we keep information, and deleting it

  • Document photos: not kept at all — deleted as soon as you confirm the extracted dates.
  • On your phone: notes, tasks, habits, documents' dates and settings stay until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Our servers (your account and cloud backup): your sign-in details (name, email, profile photo), your country code, your plan and purchase information, and your private backup stay for as long as your account exists (your message token is removed when you sign out). We do not delete accounts automatically for being inactive. Each backup replaces the one before it; we do not keep older versions.
  • Activity history: the app keeps your 300 most recent activities (walks, runs, hikes); older ones are removed automatically. Routes and activity photos stay on your phone.
  • Change history: the app's timeline of adds, edits and completions keeps the latest 200 entries; older entries are removed automatically.
  • Finished tasks: a one-day task you chose not to keep is removed once its day has passed. Any other task that is over (a one-day task, or a repeating task past its end date) is kept for 30 days after its last day and then removed automatically, together with its “done” marks.
  • Deleting your account: open Settings → Search, history & backup → Delete my account in the app and type DELETE. Your account, your plan record and your cloud backup are then deleted permanently from our servers straight away, and cannot be restored. Data on your phone is removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling. Steps are also on this page.
  • To ask for deletion of anything you cannot delete from inside the app, contact us at ayatullah.akash@gmail.com.

10. Children

RemindGuard is designed for adults managing their own and their family's documents and reminders. A family member profile (including for a child) is only a name label created and managed by the signed-in account holder; children do not create their own accounts, and the app is not directed at children under 13.

11. Security

Your app data sits in the app's private storage area on your device, protected by your phone's own security (such as your lock screen). Because documents themselves are never stored, a lost phone does not expose copies of your passport or ID from RemindGuard — only the dates and short notes you chose to save. No method of storage is completely secure, so please keep your device locked and your backups in a place you trust.

12. Your choices

  • Use manual entry instead of photographing a document — always available.
  • View the app without signing in (read-only demo) before deciding.
  • Edit or delete any note, task, habit or member at any time inside the app.
  • Export your data (paid plans) to keep your own copy.
  • Turn off notifications in your device settings, or switch off the daily summary in Settings.
  • Turn on the app lock in Settings to protect the app with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
  • Sign out, clear app data, or uninstall to remove local data from the device.
